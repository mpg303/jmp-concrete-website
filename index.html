Concrete Strength Guide

How strong should your concrete be? PSI measures compressive strength — here is what each mix is typically used for:

3000 PSIResidential
Driveways, patios, sidewalks, slabs
3500 PSIResidential
Reinforced driveways, garage floors, foundations
4000 PSICommercial
Parking lots, commercial slabs & foundations
4500 PSICommercial
Industrial floors, heavy traffic
5000 PSICommercial
Structural work, heavy loads

Residential jobs typically use 3000–3500 PSI. Commercial work starts at 4000 PSI and up. Not sure which one you need? Call us at (786) 366-2018 and we'll help you pick the right mix.
